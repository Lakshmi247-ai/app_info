## 📖 Chapter 1: The Modern Money Puzzle

Meet **Rohan**, a 29-year-old working professional living in the city.

Like most of us today, Rohan rarely carries paper cash. His smartphone is his wallet. But over the last few years, without him even realizing it, Rohan's financial life slowly turned into an invisible maze:

### 🧩 The 4-Bank Jumble
Rohan maintains **four separate bank accounts**, each opened for a specific purpose:
1. **HDFC Bank:** His primary salary account where his monthly paycheck lands.
2. **ICICI Bank:** Linked to his credit card, car EMI, and big-ticket appliance purchases.
3. **SBI (State Bank of India):** An older family savings account meant for rent, utility bills, and emergency funds.
4. **Kotak / Axis Bank:** A secondary zero-balance account he opened specifically for day-to-day UPI payments and pocket expenses.

### 📱 The Multi-UPI App Maze
To make things even more chaotic, Rohan uses whichever UPI app is quickest at the counter or offers a reward:
- **Google Pay** for quick chai breaks, autos, and splitting dinner bills with friends.
- **PhonePe** to pay electricity, gas cylinder, and broadband bills.
- **Paytm** for supermarket QR codes and metro card recharges.
- **CRED UPI** to pay his credit card dues.

### 💥 The Month-End Nightmare: "Where on Earth Did My Salary Go?"

One Sunday evening, Rohan sat down to figure out how much money he had left for the month and how much he had managed to save.

He unlocked his phone, and the headache began:
- He had to remember **4 different net banking passwords** and **4 separate MPINs**.
- Every banking app took 15 to 30 seconds to open, required SMS verification or OTPs, and pelted him with promotional pop-ups for personal loans and credit cards.
- His salary account showed only ₹18,000 left. But wait—did he transfer ₹15,000 to his Kotak UPI account, or did he spend it on shopping? Did his rent clear from SBI? How much did he spend on Swiggy and Zomato across the three different UPI apps combined?

To get a single clear answer, Rohan had to:
- Download three password-protected PDF bank statements.
- Try to reconcile UPI reference numbers across Google Pay, Paytm, and PhonePe.
- Manually calculate his total spending with a pocket calculator.

After 45 minutes of squinting at cryptic statement codes like `UPI/DR/42890123/SWIGGY` and `NEFT/N10293/TRANSFER`, Rohan threw his hands up in frustration:

> *"Why is it so effortless to spend money with a single tap, but so impossibly frustrating to see all my spending in one consolidated place?"*

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (In Plain English)

#### **1. What if my bank doesn't send an SMS for a transaction?**
This is a very common question! Today, some banks skip sending individual SMS messages for very small micro-payments (such as a ₹10 or ₹20 tea via UPI) to reduce telecom congestion, or they might send a daily consolidated summary instead.

Here is how you are covered with MySpends:
- **Quick Manual Entry (Takes 5 Seconds):** Simply tap the floating **"+" (Add Transaction)** button on your dashboard. Enter the amount, pick the bank account and category (e.g., *Food & Dining*), and tap Save.
- **Enable Full SMS Alerts in Your Bank App:** Most banks (like HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis) let you enable instant SMS alerts for all transactions regardless of amount. You can turn this on with a single toggle in your official net-banking or mobile banking app under *"Alerts & Notification Preferences"*.
- **Daily / Periodic Summary SMS:** When your bank sends an end-of-day or statement alert, MySpends can capture and balance your totals.

---

#### **2. If I delete an SMS from my phone's message inbox, will it disappear from MySpends too?**
**No, your data is completely safe.** Once MySpends detects and records a transaction from an SMS, that record is permanently stored inside MySpends' private on-device vault. Deleting, clearing, or archiving text messages in your default messaging app will **never** delete your transactions or financial reports in MySpends.

---

#### **3. What if MySpends guesses the wrong category for a shop?**
While MySpends automatically recognizes hundreds of popular merchants (Swiggy, Amazon, Uber, Reliance, etc.), local stores might sometimes have generic names.
- **One-Tap Re-categorize:** Just tap on any transaction in your list, choose the category you prefer, and hit save.
- **It Learns Your Preference:** MySpends has a smart rule engine. Once you tell it that a particular shop or UPI handle is *"Groceries"*, it remembers your rule and automatically categorizes it correctly the next time you shop there!

---

#### **4. I have 4 bank accounts and multiple UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). Do I need to manually configure them?**
**Not at all!** You don't need to do any manual setup. Whenever *any* bank sends an SMS indicating a debit or credit, MySpends reads the bank name and the last 4 digits of the account number automatically. It creates individual buckets for each bank account on its own and tracks everything together in one unified feed.

---

#### **5. Can MySpends move, withdraw, or transfer money from my accounts?**
**Never.** MySpends has **zero access** to your banking portals, net-banking credentials, or UPI PINs. It is strictly a smart "reader" that acts as a digital diary for the notifications your bank already sends to your phone. It has no technical ability to initiate payments or touch your money.

---

#### **6. Will MySpends read my private WhatsApp messages, family texts, or secret OTPs?**
**Strictly no.** MySpends is programmed with ironclad privacy filters:
- It **completely ignores** personal messages from friends, family, and contacts.
- It strictly filters out One-Time Passwords (OTPs), login codes, and two-factor verification texts.
- It only processes transactional debit and credit alerts coming from verified banking sender IDs (such as *HDFCBK*, *SBIINB*, *ICICIB*, etc.).

---

#### **7. Does MySpends upload my financial data to the internet or cloud servers?**
**No.** All transaction details, category splits, and account balances remain 100% on your device inside a local, encrypted database. 
- You can turn on Airplane Mode or turn off your Wi-Fi and mobile data, and MySpends will continue working flawlessly.
- Because your data never leaves your phone, we cannot sell your information, and you will never receive annoying loan or credit card sales calls.

---

#### **8. Does the app drain my phone’s battery by constantly running in the background?**
**Not at all.** MySpends does not run heavy background tasks that drain your battery. It uses Android's native alert system: it stays asleep 99.9% of the day, wakes up for a split second only when a bank SMS arrives to record the numbers, and immediately goes back to sleep.

---

#### **9. How does it handle cash withdrawals and cash spending?**
- **ATM Withdrawals:** When you withdraw cash from an ATM, your bank sends an SMS like *"INR 5,000 debited at ATM"*. MySpends logs this automatically under the *Cash & ATM* category.
- **Spending That Cash:** Whenever you pay someone in cash, you can quickly tap the **"+"** button to record the specific purchase, ensuring your cash tracking is just as precise as your digital spending.

---

#### **10. What happens if someone borrows my phone to look at photos or watch a video?**
You can turn on **Biometric Lock** (Fingerprint or Face Unlock) in the app settings. Whenever anyone tries to open MySpends, the app prompts for your biometric verification. Your account balances and spending habits remain completely confidential.

#### **11. What if I buy a new phone or want to open my data in Excel?**
Because MySpends does not store your records in the cloud, you remain the sole owner of your data. You can tap **Export** in the app at any time to generate a clean **CSV or Excel file**. You can email it to yourself, save it to Google Drive.

> **Note on Personal Evaluation:** MySpends is designed strictly for **personal financial evaluation**, spending habit awareness, and self-directed budgeting. It empowers you to understand your own daily cash flow, but is not an official banking portal or chartered accounting tool.

---

#### **12. How does the Financial Health Score work and how can I boost it?**
Your Financial Health Score is a transparent 0 to 100 benchmark calculated locally on your phone using four pillars:
1. **Budget Discipline:** Keeping monthly expenses safely below your defined budget limits.
2. **Savings Ratio:** Ensuring a healthy portion of monthly income remains unspent.
3. **Bill Discipline:** Staying ahead of recurring bills and avoiding overdue obligations.
4. **Spend Stability:** Maintaining steady spending rhythms without sudden erratic spikes.

You can boost your score by setting realistic category budgets, logging all major obligations under Reminders, and pacing lifestyle spends across the month.

---

#### **13. How do Bill Reminders and the Auto-Match feature work?**
When you create a reminder (or let MySpends detect recurring subscriptions from your SMS), the app schedules a gentle reminder at **9:00 AM** on or before your chosen due date.
- **Auto-Match Intelligence:** When you make the payment, your bank sends a debit SMS. MySpends detects that the merchant and amount match an active reminder, **automatically marks the current cycle as PAID**, and cancels the upcoming notification.
- **Accidental Mark as Paid?** You can easily tap the reminder in the app to edit details or toggle it back to unpaid whenever needed.
