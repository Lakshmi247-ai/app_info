❓ Plain English FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, SMS alerts, and your data.

🔍
What if my bank doesn't send an SMS for a transaction? +

This is very common! Some banks skip sending SMS for small micro-payments (like a ₹10 chai via UPI) to save telecom costs, or they send an end-of-day summary instead.

Here is how MySpends keeps you covered:

  • 5-Second Manual Entry: Tap the floating + button on your dashboard, type the amount, pick the category, and save it instantly.
  • Turn on Bank SMS Alerts: Most major banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Kotak, Axis) allow you to turn on instant SMS alerts for all transactions in your official mobile banking settings under Alerts & Notifications.
  • Daily Summaries: When your bank sends a daily debit summary SMS, MySpends reads it and helps keep your totals balanced.
If I delete an SMS from my phone's message inbox, will it disappear from MySpends? +

No, your records are completely safe. Once MySpends parses and logs a transaction, it is stored permanently in your local on-device database. Deleting or clearing text messages from your Android Messages app will never erase your transactions or charts in MySpends.

What if MySpends assigns the wrong category to a store? +

You can change it with one tap! Simply open the transaction, pick the right category, and tap save.

Smart Learning Engine: When you update a merchant's category, MySpends automatically remembers your choice so future transactions at that store are categorized correctly every time.

I have 4 bank accounts and multiple UPI apps. Do I have to configure each one? +

Zero setup needed! Whenever any bank sends a transaction notification, MySpends automatically detects the bank name, the last 4 digits of your account, and whether it was a UPI transfer or card swipe. It creates individual buckets for each bank automatically.

Can MySpends move, withdraw, or transfer money from my accounts? +

Never. MySpends has zero connection to your bank credentials, net banking passwords, or UPI PINs. It is strictly a smart reader that takes notes from the SMS messages your bank already sends to your phone. It cannot initiate payments.

Does MySpends read my private personal chats or secret OTPs? +

Strictly no. MySpends has built-in safety filters that ignore personal chats, family messages, two-factor OTPs, and promotional spam. It only processes verified transaction alerts from official banking sender IDs.

Does MySpends upload my financial data to cloud servers? +

No. All your financial data, account numbers, and transaction logs stay 100% on your device in your private local database. You can turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data completely, and MySpends will continue working normally without any issues.

Does MySpends drain my phone's battery in the background? +

Not at all. MySpends doesn't run constant background polling. It stays completely asleep, wakes up for a millisecond only when a bank SMS arrives to record the numbers, and goes right back to sleep.

11. What if I buy a new phone or want to open my data in Excel? +

You are the sole owner of your data. Because MySpends does not store your records in the cloud, you can tap the Export button in the app at any time to generate a clean CSV or Excel file. You can email it to yourself, save it to Google Drive.

Note: MySpends is built strictly for personal evaluation, financial self-awareness, and personal expense tracking. It helps you see and understand your daily cash flow with ease, without acting as an official statutory audit or banking service.
