Privacy Policy for MySpends

Package: com.orbit.myspends
Effective Date: October 1, 2026

MySpends ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to uncompromising personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict data handling practices, permissions usage, and adherence to Google Play developer policies for the MySpends mobile application.

Core Guarantee: MySpends is designed as a 100% offline, on-device financial management tool. Your bank transaction records, SMS messages, account details, categories, and financial statistics never leave your device under any circumstances. We do not operate remote servers for financial storage, and we never collect, track, or sell your personal data.

1. Architecture & Privacy Principles

  • Local Storage Only: All personal financial information, expenses, and parsed messages are stored in an encrypted, sandboxed SQLite Room database on your physical device.
  • Zero Cloud Databases: We have zero external servers receiving or storing your transaction history, account balances, or personal profiles.
  • No User Account or Registration: You do not need to sign up, provide phone numbers, submit email addresses, or log into any server to use MySpends.
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers & Ads: The app does not bundle advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob), tracking frameworks, or third-party behavioral analytics.

2. Permissions Requested & Data Usage

A. SMS Permissions (READ_SMS & RECEIVE_SMS)

Strict Purpose: Automatically parsing incoming and historical bank transaction alerts to record debits, credits, account balances, and UPI payment references without tedious manual data entry.

  • Financial-Only Scope: Our parsing engine parses messages solely from recognized financial institutions (e.g., HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, Union Bank, and UPI services).
  • Non-Financial Messages Ignored: Personal chats, two-factor OTPs, verification codes, and unrelated SMS notifications are strictly ignored and never recorded.
  • 100% On-Device Processing: SMS reading and regex categorization occur completely within the app's local sandbox. SMS content is never transmitted across the network.

B. Biometric Authentication (USE_BIOMETRIC)

Purpose: Optional App Lock feature (Fingerprint / Face Unlock) to secure your expense data when launching or resuming the application.

  • Authentication is handled strictly by the native Android OS BiometricPrompt hardware interface.
  • MySpends receives only a cryptographic success/failure callback. The app never accesses, stores, or processes raw biometric samples or templates.

C. Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Purpose: Delivering local spending alerts, monthly budget limits warnings, and daily financial wisdom reminders.

  • All notifications are scheduled and dispatched locally on your device using Android's WorkManager framework.
  • No remote push notification servers (e.g., FCM tracking servers) are utilized to track your device.

D. Internet Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Transparency Disclosure: MySpends requests network access strictly for two lightweight client-side features:

  1. Foreign Currency Exchange Rates: Querying public, non-authenticated exchange rates so you can view international expenses converted to local currency.
  2. Dynamic Bank Icons: Loading public brand logos and category icons from public CDNs.

Zero Data Transmission: At no point is your financial data, parsed SMS text, account balance, user identity, or device telemetry transmitted over the network.

E. Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION - Optional)

Purpose: Optionally tagging the geographical location where a manual transaction was logged to populate the interactive spending heatmap. Location data remains entirely on-device and is never shared.

3. Data Retention, Export & Deletion Rights

  • User Data Ownership: You retain complete ownership of all data entered or parsed by MySpends.
  • Data Export: You can export your full transaction log to a standard .csv spreadsheet anytime directly from the Analytics screen.
  • Right to Complete Erasure: You can delete any transaction individually, clear bank-specific data from Settings, or permanently erase all stored records by uninstalling the application or clearing App Data via Android Settings.

4. Children's Privacy

MySpends is a personal finance tool directed to individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children under 18 years of age.

5. Google Play Policy Compliance

MySpends complies fully with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play User Data Policy, and Specific SMS & Call Log Permissions Policies by ensuring that SMS permissions are utilized strictly for the core financial expense tracking functionality and handled 100% on-device.

6. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new capabilities. Any revisions will be published here with an updated "Effective Date".
